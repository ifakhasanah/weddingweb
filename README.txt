OURWEDS — CANVA-INSPIRED WEDDING WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: page structure and editable wedding content
- style.css: layout, colors, responsive styles, typography
- script.js: subtle scroll reveal effects
- images/: locally stored photo crops from the reference screenshots

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Keep index.html, style.css, script.js, and images/ together.
3. Open index.html in a modern browser.

How to customize:
- Edit names, date, venue, schedule, email, phone, and social handle in index.html.
- Replace images in images/ with your own photos while keeping the same filenames.
- Change colors in the :root section at the top of style.css.
- Fonts use Google Fonts when online, with generic fallback fonts if unavailable.

Note:
This is a close visual recreation based on the supplied screenshots, not an export of Canva's original source. The QR graphic is decorative and is not a functional QR code. Contact details and wedding information are sample content.
